Reflection Questions

These are some suggestions to get you you started in your further thinking about the readings today. It isn't an exam, and no one is looking over your shoulder! But if you'd like to share your reflections, or have any other feedback, we'd love to hear from you.

  • In what areas of your life do you find it most difficult to discern the voice of the Shepherd? What "other voices" are particularly distracting or alluring?
  • What does it mean to you that God’s people are a “great multitude that no one could count, from every nation, tribe, people and language”? How might this vision shape your understanding of the Church today?
  • What practical steps can you take this week to quiet the noise around you and more attentively listen for the Shepherd's voice?
